Home / Features
Built for Bards

Music without
compromise.

Everything from solo MIDI playback to conductor-led octets, musical arrangements, stage planning and a compact HUD—brought together for your next performance.

01 / PERFORMANCE

MIDI, as it was written

Load Standard MIDI Files (.mid/.midi), including multitrack songs. Keep full chord timing or choose reduced note modes when a dense passage needs a cleaner performance.

  • Play, pause, rewind, repeat and auto-next Solo playback
  • Track enable/disable, octave overrides, speed control
  • Visual piano roll and track diagnostics
02 / ARRANGEMENT

Fit the FFXIV keyboard

The FFXIV Optimiser helps adapt MIDI arrangements into the C3–C6 range—37 chromatic notes from MIDI 48 through 84—without altering your original file.

  • Per-track octave shifting
  • MIDI instrument and octave tag detection
  • MIDI Inspector for range, channels and note quality
03 / TOGETHER

Conductor & Follower

Make one performer the conductor, assign instruments and tracks to the others, and use the game’s native Ensemble Ready Check to cue everyone together.

  • Solo, duet, quartet and octet workflows
  • Same-PC and cross-PC party coordination
  • Same MIDI required locally on each client
04 / THE SHOW

Concert Director

Stage the next performance before anyone plays a note. Prepare the orchestra, review readiness, retry specific party members, and manage your concert order.

  • Stage Manager readiness reports
  • Setlist HOLD, break, encore and next-song preparation
  • Concert snapshot save / restore
05 / FORMATIONS

Stage, designed by you

Save performer placements or design a layout using draggable numbered dots. Optional vnavmesh walking is follower opt-in and explicitly conductor-triggered.

  • Line, arc, circle and row templates
  • Same-instance and safety validations
  • Experimental editor; automatic facing not supported
06 / COMPOSITION

Soulweaver Studio

Generate ensemble-ready MIDI arrangements, inspect track distributions and export a separate file for rehearsal without overwriting your source.

  • Solo, duet, quartet and octet arrangements
  • Track assignment and audio review
  • Original-MIDI-preserving exports
07 / YOUR COLLECTION

Smart Playlists

Organise separate Solo and Ensemble libraries, use favourites and tags, control order and keep recent songs within reach.

  • Playlist drag-and-drop ordering
  • Play Next, shuffle and repeat
  • Missing file cleanup and re-linking tools
08 / YOUR SOUND

Local audio & live MIDI

Use a local SF2 SoundFont to listen through your speakers, or connect a USB MIDI keyboard for live in-game Bard performance.

  • Speaker-only SoundFont MIDI preview
  • Live MIDI Piano controller support
  • Compact HUD and English, French, German interface
ⓘFeatures reflect the repository’s detailed v0.32 manual. The plugin manifest and release packages may have newer version numbers; check the installed build and compatibility after FFXIV or Dalamud updates.
