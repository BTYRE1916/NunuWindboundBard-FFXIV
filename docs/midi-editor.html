WINDBOUND BARD / v0.47.4

Edit your MIDI.

Shape the notes, controls and timing in a Standard MIDI file, then send the arrangement to Workbench to prepare Bard parts.

STUDIO → MIDI EditorAvailable in v0.44.0

Start with a song or a blank track

Choose Open MIDI… or New MIDI. The editor works with PPQ-based Standard MIDI format 0 and 1 files. Save as… creates the edited file at a fresh path. After that, Save updates that copy. Save and open in Workbench… hands the saved arrangement to the Bard preparation tools.

Play the piano roll by hand

GestureResult
Drag a noteMove it in time and pitch. Choose Move time only or Move pitch only to restrict the direction.
Drag either note edgeChange the start or end of the selected notes.
Drag empty spaceDraw a box around notes. Shift adds to the selection and Ctrl removes notes.
Right-click the rollAdd a note to the active track and channel.
Click the rulerMove the playback and paste cursor.
Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Delete while the pointer is over the rollSelect visible events, copy, paste at the cursor, or delete.

Choose a note grid for snap, zoom horizontally and vertically, or colour notes by track or channel. Undo and Redo hold up to 20 edits.

Shape expression and timing

Change velocity, Control Change, pitch bend, key pressure and channel pressure in the lane below the roll. Use single points, a freehand stroke or a line. The event inspector lets you edit numeric values, program changes, tempo, time signatures and marker text.

Selection tools align starts or ends, equalise note durations, transpose, scale time, quantise to the grid or place tuplets. Under Tempo, time signatures and measures, add tempo ramps, insert or remove bars, and set the song duration. Undo a trim or measure change while the document remains open.

Manage tracks and channels

Create, rename and delete tracks. Use the active track and channel to route new events or move selected events. Paste uses either the active destination or the original routing where it is available. Visibility controls what the roll shows. Mute and Solo apply to playback.

Listen and find the next section

Click the ruler and choose Play from cursor. Use your local SF2 SoundFont or a connected Windows MIDI output. Playback can pause, change speed, follow the playhead or stay on the current view. Jump by bar or marker, and enable the metronome to hear the meter.

Record a controller take

  1. Open MIDI devices and SoundFont, refresh the ports and choose an input. Select a MIDI output if you want MIDI thru to a hardware or virtual synth.
  2. Set the cursor. Enable Overdub with playback to hear existing content as you play, or record a standalone take.
  3. Click Record, play the controller and click Stop. Review the take, choose which event types to keep, and select its destination track and channel.
Install v0.47.4. Get the Dalamud installer to open the editor from the Studio tab.
