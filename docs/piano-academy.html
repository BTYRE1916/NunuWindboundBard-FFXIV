STUDIO / LEARNING

Piano Academy.

A falling-note MIDI tutor for a real keyboard: 88 keys, selectable tracks, Wait Mode, Play Along, tempo control and a detachable practice view.

A REAL PIANO. YOUR OWN PACE.

Learn songs one falling note at a time.

Piano Academy reads the original MIDI notes rather than folding everything into FFXIV's 37-note Performance range. Its falling-note tutor supports the full A0–C8 range and a connected Windows MIDI keyboard or synthesizer.

Practice and performance are separate. Piano Academy monitors local MIDI input and does not inject note events into FFXIV. For a song you want to perform in-game, return to PERFORMANCE → Songs & Playback.

Start a lesson

  1. Open STUDIO → Piano Academy. Load a MIDI from your song library or use Browse lesson MIDI.
  2. Choose a track. Select the notes you want to study and adjust the left/right split.
  3. Pick a practice mode. Wait for correct notes pauses until you play; Play Along follows time and tracks accuracy.
  4. Connect your MIDI piano. Refresh ports, select the device and enable practice monitoring.
  5. Open the large practice view. Adjust keyboard range and scroll speed; use the detachable window for a fuller view.

Wait Mode vs Play Along

01 / PRACTICE

Wait for correct notes

Learn the correct keys and chords before the lesson advances. Ideal for first-time practice and learning hand positions.

02 / TIMING

Play Along

The timeline continues at your chosen speed. Compare your played keys with the MIDI, practise timing and loop a difficult section.

Supported lesson files are Standard MIDI Files. A physical MIDI keyboard provides your local sound; optional SoundFont Preview remains a separate rehearsal feature.
