BAND / STAGE DESIGN

Take your position.

Solo positioning, optional group walking, saved formations and automatic facing for your next FFXIV Bard concert.

A PLACE FOR EVERY PERFORMER

Plan positions and facing before the curtain rises.

WindboundBard's Stage Formations can place a solo character or a participating band into a saved arrangement. Drag dots on the top-down designer, assign members to slots and set the facing angles for an audience-facing lineup.

SOLO STAGE

Just you and the audience

Save your stage anchor, choose a point and facing, then ask your Bard to walk there. No party or conductor required.

CONDUCTOR STAGE

One orchestra, one shape

Arrange a party with line, arc, circle or two-row layouts. Followers opt in before accepting stage movement commands.

Before you set positions

  1. Stop MIDI playback, exit Performance and leave combat.
  2. Ensure vnavmesh is installed and ready on every client expected to move.
  3. For ensemble movement, the party leader must be the conductor and members must permit remote stage movement.
  4. Save the current formation, or open the visual designer to edit spots and facing.
  5. Select the explicit movement command. Use STOP STAGE MOVEMENT if the stage needs a reset.
No teleporting. Walking is bounded by proximity and state checks. Facing is applied after arrival when the supported game API allows it. Avoid using stage controls while a performance is playing.

Stage formations are an experimental, patch-sensitive utility. Keep your stage rehearsal separate from a live audience until tested on your specific FFXIV and Dalamud versions.
