BAND / IDENTITIES

The band, pictured.

Capture authentic Adventurer Plate pictures and recognise performers at a glance when assigning MIDI tracks and instruments.

THE BAND BEHIND THE MUSIC

Put a face beside every name.

Band Portraits displays real FFXIV Adventurer Plate thumbnails next to performer names in the track and instrument assignment views. The image is captured manually from the game's visible Adventurer Plate and saved on your client.

  1. View an Adventurer Plate in FFXIV. Open the character's plate before clicking Capture.
  2. Open BAND → Band Portraits. This tab is available even when you're playing Solo.
  3. Select CAPTURE beside the correct member name.
  4. Crop inside the preview. Drag the mouse across the screenshot to select a square over the portrait, then save.
  5. Return to BAND → Ensemble. The portrait appears next to that member's assignments on this computer.

Conductor and follower views

The conductor can use captured images to recognise each Bard when assigning tracks and instruments. Followers can manage their own local gallery. Pictures are associated with the member's identity instead of their temporary place in the party list.

Local-only capture. The plugin does not automatically fetch other players' portraits, record video, upload images or transmit saved pictures to other clients. Open the Adventurer Plate and capture it manually.

To update an image, capture the character again. You can also remove a cached portrait from the same tab.
